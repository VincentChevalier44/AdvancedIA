# Validation — AdvancedIA HumanAI

This page says what the offline bench proves, what it cannot prove, and what is still to be seen in game. The figures
of the release bench (soak, benchmark, open criteria) are generated by `tests/report.py` in [MESURES.md](MESURES.md)
and [RELEASE_STATUS.json](RELEASE_STATUS.json).

## What the test bench checks

Figures of the Beta 1.52 chain (9 November 2026):

- **695 checks, 0 failures** (`tests/test_pdr.py`), including the **78 situations of
  Appendix A** of the PDR, each with its own test function, and a 10,000-step fuzz (fixed seed, NaN/inf, aberrant
  positions, speeds, splines, laps, dimensions and flags, up to 60 AI). The fixtures are kinematic: some freeze a
  geometry to isolate a behaviour, others reposition a target to test a transition. Globals are strict: reading or
  writing an undeclared global fails the check.
- **136 integration cases** (`tests/integration.py`): the real entry file `AdvancedIA.lua` against a simulated
  host (first installation, windows and overlay, every setting and its bounds walked through the interface, presets,
  racing rules, weather, pits, updates, CSP options, bridge, unloading and reloading).
- **1,672 mutations, all detected** (`tests/mutate.py`): each important rule is altered and its test must fail
  on the mutant while passing on the clean code. Errors caught on shutdown, on restore, on a session change and in the
  interface also fail the tests.
- AST linter (`tests/lint.py`): scopes, forbidden globals, unused locals, duplicate definitions, external `require`
  calls, and every field read from CSP structures checked against the SDK. Syntax also loaded by LuaJIT 2.1.
- Reference fingerprints (`tests/golden.py`, `tests/golden142.py`): a pure clean-up must leave them identical.
- Soak of **8 h / 1,728,000 frames / 40 AI** (`tests/run_soak.py`) on a track day with spacing, endurance, variable
  weather, classes, stints and traffic, then one hour of a race with racing rules; a 30,000-frame benchmark with 30 AI.
  The raw maximum is kept, process interruptions included: no spike is masked.

## Validation limits

A safety assertion checks every move of the core in the bench: no movement towards an occupied side, offset bounds and
at most six direction changes per minute. This does not prove the absence of collisions in the real physics engine,
between two perceptions or with another app writing at the same time.

The CSP calls in the integration tests are simulated. The 1.05 read-back of a level above 100 % (logged since Beta 1.53
as `niveau au-dessus de 100 %: sonde 1.05 relue …`), whether AC really drives faster above 100 % when the read-back is
capped, the headlight calibration and the left/right signs must produce their observations in the game; the bench
checks their branches, their restores and their safeguards.

AI spins at fixed places of a track (Nordschleife, 9 November: 14 spins in 10 min, 8 of them between 21.0 and 21.3 km)
cannot be reproduced on the bench, which has no tyre physics. Since Beta 1.54 each spin context line says the app's
speed limit, whether a lapse in focus was running and the state the AI had just left; whether the app plays a part
needs the same session with APP OFF (or the debug log) in the game.

Beta 1.55 changes AI behaviour outside the race (futile overtake attempts cut, ordinary yields only to a really faster
AI, flashes suspended after yields without a pass) and the flying-lap warning to the player; the bench reproduces the
9 November patterns, but the effect on a real track day (fewer yields, shorter attempts, a warning only for cars that
really arrive) must be checked in the game's session log (`abandons :`, `fins de cession :`, `cessions refusees`,
`appels de phares suspendus`). Whether CSP's AI rain-tyre flag changes the tyres shown, or the grip, independently of
the compound fitted is not documented by the SDK; the minute summary now counts that flag (`drapeau pluie de CSP`), and
`pneus pluie de CSP revenus sur piste seche` tells whether it comes back in the dry.

Beta 1.58 hands an AI the app was driving back to AC in the pit lane (offset ramped to zero, then the original level,
aggression, caution, grip and speed); the bench reproduces a finished car with a leftover offset and speed cap, but the
return to the boxes after a real race must be seen in game.

Beta 1.56 adds three options, all off by default (your track limits handled by the app, escalating penalties, the race
report). With the defaults the golden, race142, soak and unit-suite journals are identical to Beta 1.55. With the options
on, the bench checks the warnings, the time penalty, the AC throttle-cut request and its fallback, the escalation and the
CSV file; whether AC applies the throttle cut with Content Manager's penalties off, and what CSP reads for
`penaltiesEnabled` and `allowedTyresOut`, can only be seen in the game.

The benchmark times the **pure core**, without the SDK reads and writes or the interface. The counter of
**DIAGNOSTICS › Performance** measures the AI loop with the SDK reads and writes, without the window drawing. The total
cost including the interface must be observed with the CSP profiler.

The bench's strict criteria (raw maximum under 1 ms, less than 256 KiB of total memory variation over the whole window)
have stayed open since 1.0.0; the PDR itself asks for no growth after stabilisation, which the memory curves show. The
current status is in [MESURES.md](MESURES.md).

## In-game checks still open

The lists of Beta 1.46 and older were removed in Beta 1.52 to keep this page about the current behaviour: their points
that still apply are under *Still open from earlier versions*, and the git history keeps the full lists. The points
below have not been observed in game yet.

### Beta 1.59.1

- [ ] Practice with frequent offs at one corner: a car parked well off the track is logged `incident ecarte: arretee hors
  piste, loin de la trajectoire depuis 15 s` and its yellow flag goes; no yellow flag stays with nothing on the track.
- [ ] A car wedged against a wall on the track is towed (`IA immobile depannee a son stand (bloquee, …)`) within a minute.
- [ ] Practice or qualifying: more than 5 offences under flags give throttle cuts only, never AC's black flag.
- [ ] Passing a car that has just crashed and rejoins slowly, under another car's yellow: no penalty.
- [ ] AC's throttle-cut penalty: the overlay explains how to serve it; note how AC really counts it down (speed, pedal).

### Beta 1.59

- [ ] Short race (8 to 10 laps): fewer yellow notices than in 1.57/1.58; a car stopped or crawling on the track still
  gets a double yellow; a car parked well off the track is cleared after 15 s; a spot with repeated incidents is logged
  once (`point difficile`) and never at the start.
- [ ] First lap: careful, but no AI almost stopping in slow corners; one car with wheels on the grass does not slow the pack.
- [ ] Player pit reminder at most once per lap, before the pit entry; with SpotterEnginer 1.2.0 running, « box this lap »
  only on its lap.
- [ ] **App overlay** off: nothing drawn by AdvancedIA, AC's own flags, banners and session box back (after CSP re-reads
  gui.ini); on again: as before.
- [ ] HOME rule-set selector: the choice shows in RACING RULES; greyed once the race has started.
- [ ] Same race restarted: the end-of-race summary and the bridge's finished/final/grid are those of the new race.
- [ ] Mandatory-stop undercut: `strategie: <name> arret obligatoire avance (undercut)` (or cover/overcut) in a race with
  a mandatory stop, and the stop happens inside the window.
- [ ] **Practice and qualifying: real AI runs** (on): qualifying runs of one flying lap with box waits; practice
  programmes logged as `passage en piste:`; run fuel set in the box (`carburant du passage:`); no AI stuck in its box.
- [ ] **Varied starting strategies** (on): part of the grid starts on another dry compound, as logged; no extra stop.
- [ ] **Pit stop for damage** (on): a damaged AI still at pace pits and is repaired; it waits under a VSC; the
  thresholds feel right.
- [ ] Preset **EXPORT** writes `Documents\Assetto Corsa\AdvancedIA\AdvancedIA-presets.ini` (or says it used the CSP app
  folder); **IMPORT** after CONFIRM restores the same settings.

- [ ] A race pit stop: the AI drives down the lane to its box normally, then leaves and is driven again on track.
- [ ] F1 rules with a mandatory stop: an AI that stops on track during its requested stop and is moved to its box by AC is
