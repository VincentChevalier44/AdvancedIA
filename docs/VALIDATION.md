# Validation — AdvancedIA HumanAI

This page says what the offline bench proves, what it cannot prove, and what is still to be seen in game. The figures
of the release bench (soak, benchmark, open criteria) are generated by `tests/report.py` in [MESURES.md](MESURES.md)
and [RELEASE_STATUS.json](RELEASE_STATUS.json).

## What the test bench checks

Figures of the Beta 1.52 chain (9 November 2026):

- **695 checks, 0 failures** (`tests/test_pdr.py`), including the **78 situations of
  Appendix A** of the PDR, each with its own test function, and a 10,000-step fuzz (fixed seed, NaN/inf, aberrant
  positions, speeds, splines, laps, dimensions and flags, up to 60 AI). The fixtures are kinematic: some freeze a
  geometry to isolate a behaviour, others reposition a target to test a transition. Globals are strict: reading or
  writing an undeclared global fails the check.
- **136 integration cases** (`tests/integration.py`): the real entry file `AdvancedIA.lua` against a simulated
  host (first installation, windows and overlay, every setting and its bounds walked through the interface, presets,
  racing rules, weather, pits, updates, CSP options, bridge, unloading and reloading).
- **1,672 mutations, all detected** (`tests/mutate.py`): each important rule is altered and its test must fail
  on the mutant while passing on the clean code. Errors caught on shutdown, on restore, on a session change and in the
  interface also fail the tests.
- AST linter (`tests/lint.py`): scopes, forbidden globals, unused locals, duplicate definitions, external `require`
  calls, and every field read from CSP structures checked against the SDK. Syntax also loaded by LuaJIT 2.1.
- Reference fingerprints (`tests/golden.py`, `tests/golden142.py`): a pure clean-up must leave them identical.
- Soak of **8 h / 1,728,000 frames / 40 AI** (`tests/run_soak.py`) on a track day with spacing, endurance, variable
  weather, classes, stints and traffic, then one hour of a race with racing rules; a 30,000-frame benchmark with 30 AI.
  The raw maximum is kept, process interruptions included: no spike is masked.

## Validation limits

A safety assertion checks every move of the core in the bench: no movement towards an occupied side, offset bounds and
at most six direction changes per minute. This does not prove the absence of collisions in the real physics engine,
between two perceptions or with another app writing at the same time.

The CSP calls in the integration tests are simulated. Native detection of a level above 100 %, the headlight
calibration and the left/right signs must produce their observations in the game; the bench checks their branches,
their restores and their safeguards.

The benchmark times the **pure core**, without the SDK reads and writes or the interface. The counter of
**DIAGNOSTICS › Performance** measures the AI loop with the SDK reads and writes, without the window drawing. The total
cost including the interface must be observed with the CSP profiler.

The bench's strict criteria (raw maximum under 1 ms, less than 256 KiB of total memory variation over the whole window)
have stayed open since 1.0.0; the PDR itself asks for no growth after stabilisation, which the memory curves show. The
current status is in [MESURES.md](MESURES.md).

## In-game checks still open

The lists of Beta 1.46 and older were removed in Beta 1.52 to keep this page about the current behaviour: their points
that still apply are under *Still open from earlier versions*, and the git history keeps the full lists. The points
below have not been observed in game yet.

### Beta 1.52

- [ ] After a session change or an app reload, with the Safety Car held in its pit box (or an AI parked): no more line
  « voiture de securite: retenue a son emplacement » every 5 s; one line at most, then nothing until it is released.
- [ ] Flying laps (practice, qualifying): the reason logged when a car is not on a flying lap matches what happens
  (tour de sortie, refroidissement, tour de rentree, voiture endommagee, trop lente); after an aborted lap the « de
  suite » count starts again; no `tour lance` line in a race or on a track day.
- [ ] SpotterEnginer installed, with a newer SpotterEnginer release published: about 10 s after loading, **HOME › TO
  CHECK** shows *SpotterEnginer x.y available (installed: a.b)*; **SpotterEnginer ›** opens SpotterEnginer's window;
  SpotterEnginer up to date: no line. Without SpotterEnginer: no line, no read.
- [ ] F1 race: a stop made just before the window closes, with the compound change still pending, is not penalised
  once the compound has changed.
- [ ] Practice then race in the same weekend: an AI repaired in practice is repaired again at its first race stop.

### Beta 1.51

- [ ] New session (automatic profile): `preset_session: <name> (profil <P>)` once per session; your changes kept until
  the next session; a custom preset loaded into the profile (`Perso`) never replaced.
- [ ] Practice and qualifying: an AI leaving the pits never forces its way past you on a flying lap
  (`cede a <name> en tour lance`); the pit-exit hold waits for flying cars only.
- [ ] Qualifying: AI cool-down laps after one or two flying laps (`refroidissements des IA` in the minute summary).

### Beta 1.50

- [ ] Race start (25 cars): no AI lifting or braking on the straight for no reason; minute summary « ligne droite : pied
  tenu N fois »; every remaining « radar avant: … » line of the start gives a reason in brackets.
- [ ] Safety Car: « retenue a son emplacement (attente d AC) » at the session start; at a deployment « liberee, sort par
  la voie des stands », « sortie de son stand apres X s », « sortie en piste devant <name> », « en tete de la file »,
  « rentre ce tour »; it is seen driving out of the pit lane. « ne quitte pas son emplacement » means AC did not release
  it: report it.
- [ ] Red-flag relaunch: the Safety Car leaves first, the overlay says « Attendez » until then, the AI follow in order.
- [ ] SpotterEnginer: `scOut`, `scLeading`, `restartPlace`, `restartRetake` received once each; the « change compound »
  reminder (`pitCompound`) announced once per approach, even when braking before the pit entry.

### Beta 1.49

- [ ] F1 race, player stop on another compound: `arret aux stands du joueur compte: compose change (X -> Y, …)`,
  overlay *Mandatory stop validated*; same compound: `non compte: compose inchange (X -> X)` and the 8 s notice.
- [ ] Old `apps/lua/ACAdvancedIA/manifest.ini` put back (test only): AdvancedIA shows the instruction, logs `ancienne
  version ACAdvancedIA encore installee` and drives nothing.

### Beta 1.48

- [ ] Red flag in a race: `drapeau rouge: ordre fige (N voitures)`; the overlay shows **RESTART P…** in the pits.
- [ ] Restart: `voiture de securite: posee en piste a …` only as a fallback; the AI leave in the frozen order.
- [ ] Retaking a place behind a car classed behind you: `reprise: … reprend sa place sur …`, no penalty.
- [ ] F1 red flag on lap 1: the tyre change counts as the mandatory stop.

### Beta 1.47

- [ ] Monza and Spa race starts (F1 rules, 20+ AI): no wave of `radar avant` lines in the first 10 s; no chain of rear
  contacts at the first braking zone.
- [ ] Careful first lap: `premier tour: mode attentif pour N IA (depart)` at the start and `fin du mode attentif apres X
  s` within one lap; from lap 2 the AI fight as before.
- [ ] Red flag: never for a queue behind an accident (`pas de drapeau rouge: … dans la file`); only when crashed cars
  really block the track.
- [ ] `defense:` lines only against a follower clearly to one side, at most 20 km/h faster, on a straight.
- [ ] Overtakes: « cible plus rapide » below 40 % of the attempts, the same pair at most twice per 10 min.

### Still open from earlier versions

- [ ] Physical validation with Pure (weather read from CSP only).
- [ ] CSP functions only verified offline: HUD flag, forced stop, AI headlight flashes, parked car, return to the pits.
- [ ] Track light panels: white (3) and orange (6), marked *does not work yet* in the SDK.
- [ ] Tyre wear scale: `usure des pneus: echelle verifiee sur X (…)` once per race session; note how AC reports
  `tyreWear`.
- [ ] CSP AI flood beyond 2 km of range.

These boxes stay open until actually observed. The automated tests are not enough to certify the physics and FPS
criteria.
